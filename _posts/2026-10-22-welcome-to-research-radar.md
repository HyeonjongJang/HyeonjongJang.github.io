**Research Radar** is where I take one notable AI/ML paper and explain it properly.

Each post works through a single paper from its full text: the problem it attacks, the core idea in plain intuition (with an analogy or two), how the mechanism actually works, the central math with every symbol explained, the headline results against their baselines, and an honest look at what the paper does and doesn't show.

The aim is that you can learn the paper here without opening the PDF first. Selection leans toward my own interests: large language models and their systems, automatic heuristic design, multi-agent path finding (MAPF), and adaptive search control.
