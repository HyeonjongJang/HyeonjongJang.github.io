**Research Radar** is a running log of what catches my eye in AI/ML research and open source.

Each entry picks a few notable GitHub repositories and papers, says in plain terms what is new and why it matters, and adds a one-line take. Every claim is tied to its source, and each item links back to the original.

This is a curated adjunct to my [research work](/publications/) — not a replacement for it. Posts here lean toward my own interests: automatic heuristic design with large language models, multi-agent path finding (MAPF), and adaptive search control.
